Anh Duc Tran and Anh-Quang Vu contributed equally, with Loc Pham, Trong Hieu Nguyen, Chuong Nguyen Le, Quang Huy Dao, Hoang Vu Dao, Van-Truong Nguyen, Chuong Nguyen, Pham Tuyen Le, and Quan Nguyen.
