Motion 2 is the second-generation humanoid. The design target is a robot that can keep working: agile enough for dynamic motion, and able to swap its own battery in about a minute so a shift does not end at the charger. I contribute to the motion side of that system.

## CES 2026

Motion 2 launched publicly at CES 2026 in Las Vegas, 6–9 January, at LVCC West Hall, booth 5001, with Qualcomm. Live cycles covered:

- conversational interaction
- extreme range of motion and balancing
- whole-body teleoperation
- dynamic motion: walking, boxing, and dancing

The booth drew industry visitors including Euisun Chung of Hyundai and Cristiano Amon of Qualcomm, and the team spoke with Yahoo Finance about taking humanoids from the lab onto a real floor. The point of the demo was not a single trick. It was stability, full-body control, and a robot that can stay in operation.

## What “nonstop” means here

VinMotion’s introduction of Motion 2 centers on one-minute battery hot-swap, so the robot can go back to work without a long charge cycle. That only matters if the motion stack survives contact, fast moves, and the hand-off between teleoperation and autonomous balance. CES was the first time that package was shown outside Vietnam.
